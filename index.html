Chapter 8

The Mom & Dad Candy Tax

Uh oh. Something sneaky happens while you're at school or fast asleep…

Wait, what's a tax?

A tax is when you get to keep most of what you earn, but a small piece goes to someone else to help pay for things. When grown-ups earn money at work, a piece of it goes to the city and the country as taxes. That pays for schools, roads, parks, fire trucks and libraries that everybody shares.

The Mom & Dad Candy Tax works the same way. You worked hard for that candy, so you keep most of it. Mom and Dad each take a tiny piece to "pay" for all the stuff they did to help.

Cut the candy into 8 slices

You keep 6 slicesMom: 1 sliceDad: 1 slice

One slice out of 8 is called one eighth. Mom gets one eighth, Dad gets one eighth, so together they take 2 out of 8 slices. That's the same as one quarter, like a quarter of a pizza.

What Mom & Dad's tax pays for

  • 🎃Buying the costume (and fixing it when it rips)
  • 🚗Driving to 10 different candy events
  • 🔦Flashlight duty in the dark
  • 🎒Carrying the bucket when it got too heavy
  • 🧮Counting all 2,133 pieces into a spreadsheet
  • 🦷Protecting your teeth (very important job)

The tax calculator

If the candy doesn't split evenly into 8, the leftovers go to you. Parents can't tax crumbs.

The good news: tax-free candy!

Some candies are so weird that Mom and Dad don't want them. That makes them tax-free, so you keep every single one: Toxic Waste (way too sour for grown-ups), "flale" (nobody knows what it is), "sweetart rpes" (Mom can't even read it), and the Honey Straws (we never found any, so there's nothing to tax).
